# Peak Cycles Returns Policy

## Bikes
Complete bikes can be returned within **14 days** of delivery only if they are **unridden** (no tyre wear, reflectors and protective film still on). A 10% restocking fee applies and the buyer pays return freight ($120 flat).

## Ridden bikes
Ridden bikes are **not returnable**. Instead we offer a free fit adjustment and one free size exchange within 30 days.

## Parts and accessories
Unused parts and accessories: 30 days, full refund. Installed parts, tubeless sealant and nutrition are non-returnable.
