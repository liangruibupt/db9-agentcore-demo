# Warranty

## Nimbus-brand products
All Nimbus-brand gear carries a **lifetime warranty** against manufacturing defects (seams, zippers, buckles, delamination). Normal wear and accidental damage are not covered, but we offer at-cost repairs.

## Third-party brands
Third-party products follow the manufacturer's warranty. We help file the claim.

## How to claim
Send photos of the defect and your order id through the support chat. Approved claims get a replacement or repair within 14 days.
