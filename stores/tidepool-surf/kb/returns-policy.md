# Tidepool Surf Co. Returns

## Surfboards
Boards can be returned within **7 days** if unwaxed and undinged. Once a board has been waxed or surfed it cannot be returned, but you can trade it in toward another board at 60% of the price paid within 30 days.

## Wetsuits
Wetsuits can be exchanged for another size within **45 days**, even if worn once in the water, as long as they are rinsed and undamaged. Refunds (not exchanges) are store credit only.

## Rentals
Rental bookings can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup.
