# Shipping & Delivery

## Options
- Standard (3-7 business days): free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95.
- Express (1-2 business days): $19.95. Order before 2 pm Pacific for same-day dispatch.
- Locker pickup: free, available in Seattle, Portland and Vancouver BC.

## International
We ship to Canada, Japan and the EU. Duties are prepaid at checkout (DDP) so there are no surprise fees on delivery.

## Lost or damaged parcels
Report within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We reship once at no cost; a second loss is refunded.
